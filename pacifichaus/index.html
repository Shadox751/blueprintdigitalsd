Working with us

What it's like to work with Pacific Haus.

Remodels go wrong when nobody knows the cost, the schedule or who's in charge. We put all three in writing.

An estimate you can read

Every line written out: materials, labor, permits. If something changes, you'll see it on a signed change order before we do the work.

A plan with dates on it

You get a written schedule before work starts, so you know when the crew arrives and roughly when your kitchen is back.

An update every Friday

What got done this week, what's next, and anything we need you to decide. No chasing us for news.

A family you'll actually meet

When you call, you talk to one of us. The people you meet at the consultation are the people responsible for your project.
