Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective September 10, 2026

This policy explains what information Pacific Haus Improvements ("we," "us") collects through pacifichausimprovements.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you request an estimate: your name, phone number, email address, the ZIP code of the property, your project type and timeline, anything you write about the project, and your contact and text-message preferences.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages viewed and the type of device and browser. Our analytics are cookieless and aggregated. They do not identify you personally or follow you to other websites.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule a visit and prepare an estimate.
  • To communicate with you about your project.
  • To keep a record of requests we receive and to improve our website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run this website and handle requests. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Website hosting and cookieless analytics (Vercel)
  • Email delivery (Resend) and business email and document storage (Google Workspace)
  • Instant notifications to our team's phones (Pushover)

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text messages

We only send text messages about your project if you check the consent box on our form. Consent is not required to get an estimate. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

Do Not Track

We do not track visitors across other websites, so our site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Reviewing or correcting your information

To review, correct or delete the information you've given us, email info@pacifichausimprovements.com or call (858) 902-4630.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
