Three ways in

Most kitchen projects fall into one of these.

You don't need to know which one yours is. We'll figure it out together at the first visit.

Refresh

Keep the layout you have. New countertops, backsplash, sink and faucet, lighting, and refinished or refaced cabinets. The quickest route to a kitchen that feels new.

Usually involves: counters, tile, fixtures, cabinet finish, lighting.

Full remodel

Same footprint, everything new: cabinets, counters, flooring and lighting, with your appliances installed and the permits handled.

Usually involves: demolition, cabinets, electrical, plumbing, permits.

Custom & layout change

Open a wall, move the sink, add an island or rework the whole plan. This is where structural, plumbing and electrical changes come in.

Usually involves: framing, engineering where needed, relocated utilities, more permitting.

Questions we hear

Kitchen remodel FAQ

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope and on how long cabinets and counters take to arrive. You'll get a written schedule with your estimate, and we update it every Friday once work starts.

Do you pull the permits?

Yes. When the work needs a permit, we file it and schedule the inspections. Depending on your address, that's the City of San Diego, your own city's building department, or the County.

How much is the deposit?

California law caps the down payment on a home improvement contract at $1,000 or 10% of the contract price, whichever is less. After that, payments are tied to work that's been completed.

Can we stay in the house during the remodel?

Usually, yes. We'll talk through a temporary kitchen setup with you and keep the work area closed off and cleaned up.

Do I need a design before I call?

No. Bring photos of kitchens you like, or nothing at all. We work out the layout and materials with you before we write the estimate.
