About us

A family company from San Diego.

Pacific Haus started in 2026 with a simple idea: a remodel should come with straight answers. What will it cost, how long will it take, and who is in charge?

Why we do it this way

When you call, you talk to one of us.

We're a family business, and we run it like one. Miguel Gutierrez, our licensed general contractor, leads every build. Allan and Emilio Constantino manage projects day to day, and Sandra Constantino takes care of our clients from the first call. The people who answer your call are the same people responsible for your project.

We write our estimates line by line, put the scope and schedule in writing, and send an update every Friday while we work. It isn't complicated. It's just what we'd want in our own homes.

We're based in San Diego and work across San Diego County and every county that borders it: Orange, Riverside and Imperial.
