Bathroom remodeling in San Diego County

From a new vanity and tile to a full gut with a walk-in shower. Small rooms have a lot of trades packed into them, and we coordinate all of them.

Get a bathroom estimate How it works
Two ways in

Update it, or take it down to the studs.

Update

Keep the tub or shower where it is and replace what you see: vanity, counter, mirror, lighting, fixtures and tile. Faster, and less disruptive to the rest of the house.

Full remodel

Everything comes out. New plumbing where it's needed, proper waterproofing behind the tile, a new shower or tub, ventilation and lighting. Permits handled.

Questions we hear

Bathroom remodel FAQ

Can you turn my tub into a walk-in shower?

In most bathrooms, yes. We check the drain location, the floor and the wall framing at the first visit and tell you what the change involves before you commit.

What if you find water damage behind the tile?

We show you what we found, explain the fix and write it up as a change order with the cost. Nothing extra gets done without your signature.

How much is the deposit?

California law caps the down payment at $1,000 or 10% of the contract price, whichever is less. After that, payments are tied to completed work.
