Our process

How a Pacific Haus project works

Five steps, in the order they happen. You'll always know what's next, what it costs and who to call.

  1. Consultation

    We visit your home, look at the space, take measurements and listen to what you want. We also talk about budget early, so the design fits it.

    You getA clear sense of what is possible and what it involves.

  2. Design & estimate

    We work out the layout and materials with you, then write up the estimate. Every item has its own line: materials, labor, permits.

    You getAn itemized written estimate and a proposed schedule.

  3. Contract & permits

    You sign a contract with the full scope and a payment schedule. We order materials and file for any permits the work needs.

    You getA signed scope, a payment schedule and a start date.

  4. Build

    The crew works from the plan. If something unexpected comes up, you get a written change order to approve before we do the extra work.

    You getA written update every Friday: what got done, what is next, and anything we need from you.

  5. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished space with you, make a punch list of anything that is not right, and finish it before we call the job done.

    You getA finished room and your warranty terms in writing.

Money

How payments work

A small down payment

California law caps it at $1,000 or 10% of the contract price, whichever is less. We follow it to the letter.

Payments follow the work

The payment schedule in your contract is tied to stages of completed work. You never pay far ahead of what's been built.

No surprise charges

If the scope changes, you see it on a signed change order with the cost before the work happens.
