Neighborhoods

Every part of town has its own kind of house.

Eastlake

Master-planned neighborhoods built from the late 1980s on. Plenty of homes here still have their original builder-grade cabinets and counters.

Otay Ranch

Newer homes, mostly from the late 1990s onward. A common goal is swapping builder-basic finishes for something that feels like yours.

Rancho del Rey

Homes from the 1980s and 1990s, where opening up a closed-off kitchen is a frequent request.

West Chula Vista

Older homes, many from the 1950s to the 1970s. Remodels here often include updating electrical and plumbing along the way.

Permits

City or County, we handle it.

Remodels inside Chula Vista city limits are permitted through the City's Development Services Department. Homes in unincorporated areas nearby, like Bonita, go through the County of San Diego instead. We work out which applies to your address and file the paperwork.

HOAs

We check before we schedule.

Many Eastlake and Otay Ranch homes are in HOAs. Interior work usually doesn't need HOA approval, but anything visible from outside can. We'll go over it with you before work is scheduled.
