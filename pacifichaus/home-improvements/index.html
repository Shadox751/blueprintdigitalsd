What we do

Around the house

Painting

Interior walls, ceilings, trim, doors and cabinets, plus exterior repaints. Prep done properly so the finish lasts.

Flooring

Luxury vinyl plank, laminate, tile and engineered wood, including removing the old floor and leveling what is underneath.

Cabinets & countertops

Replace or reface cabinets, swap out countertops, or add hardware and lighting without a full remodel.

Fixtures & appliances

Faucets, sinks, lighting, ceiling fans, vent hoods and appliances installed and connected.

Drywall & repairs

Patching, texture matching, damaged drywall, doors and trim. The list of small things you have been meaning to get done.

Installations

Already bought it? Tell us the make and model and we will quote the install.

Thinking bigger?

Small jobs often turn into a plan.

If new floors have you looking at the kitchen, we can price both and phase the work so it makes sense for your budget.
